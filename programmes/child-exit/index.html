CHILD-EXIT

From immediate protection to sustainable exit.

CHILD-EXIT is a research and intervention-development framework addressing a central question in the protection of minors affected by forced criminality: what conditions make it possible for a child not only to leave a criminal network, but to remain outside it over time?

“Protection is not the end point. The policy question is whether the child remains out.”

The policy gap

Removal does not necessarily mean exit.

Children exploited through criminal activities may be identified, removed from immediate danger and recognised as victims while remaining exposed to conditions that make re-recruitment possible.

CHILD-EXIT therefore shifts the focus from the single moment of removal towards the trajectory that follows protection: the construction of credible alternatives, the conditions of return and reintegration, and the capacity of a new trajectory to endure.

A public conceptual pathway

From identification to sustainable exit

01 · Identify Recognise exploitation and forced criminality behind apparent offending.
02 · Protect Secure the child and apply child-centred protection and non-punishment principles.
03 · Build an alternative Make another trajectory credible, accessible and socially sustainable.
04 · Sustain the exit Examine whether protection can become durable disengagement over time.

Position scientifique

Un cadre en développement, pas un standard établi

CHILD-EXIT n'est pas présenté comme une intervention validée, un protocole standardisé ou un modèle dont l'efficacité serait démontrée.

Le CIRITS développe ce cadre comme une proposition de recherche destinée à être confrontée à la littérature scientifique, aux pratiques professionnelles, aux politiques publiques et à l'évaluation empirique.

Son développement repose sur le dialogue avec des chercheurs, professionnels de la protection de l'enfance, acteurs de la lutte contre la traite, institutions judiciaires et organisations internationales.
